Mission

Why we built Ferne

Menopause is one of the most significant transitions in a woman's life — yet it remains one of the most under-discussed, under-researched, and under-supported health experiences in medicine.

Women often spend years experiencing symptoms without understanding the patterns, connections, or triggers that drive them. Doctor appointments are short. Memories are imperfect. Patterns go unnoticed.

Ferne was built to change that. By helping women track their symptoms consistently and understand them deeply, we give them the clarity and confidence to take control — and the data to have more meaningful conversations with their healthcare providers.

Our name comes from the fern — a plant that thrives in even the most challenging environments, resilient and quietly beautiful. That's what we believe every woman experiencing menopause can be.
