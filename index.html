Features

Everything you need to
understand your journey

Built specifically for women experiencing perimenopause and menopause — because you deserve more than a generic health app.

🌡️

Hot Flash Tracking

Log intensity, duration, and triggers instantly. Discover patterns that help you predict and manage hot flashes before they disrupt your day.

📊

Daily Check-In

Track sleep quality, mood, energy, and stress in under 60 seconds. Build a rich picture of how you feel day-to-day over time.

🤖

AI Health Insights

Our AI analyzes your patterns and delivers personalized insights — like how caffeine, stress, or sleep are connected to your symptoms.

📄

Doctor PDF Report

Generate a comprehensive, shareable PDF of your symptom history to bring to your next appointment. Your doctor will thank you.

🩸

Period & Cycle Tracking

Monitor your cycle changes during perimenopause. Track patterns, irregularities, and how your cycle connects to other symptoms.

⚡

Trigger Identification

Log food, lifestyle, environmental, and emotional triggers. Understand what makes your symptoms better or worse — and take back control.
